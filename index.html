Welcome message

From the organizing committee

Dear colleagues, it is a pleasure to welcome you to the Yonsei Endoscopic Ear Surgery Workshop.

Over three days we move from the middle ear, through the facial nerve and the internal auditory canal, to the infratemporal fossa. The lateral skull base is where neurootology, neurosurgery and head and neck surgery meet: its anatomy is dense and unforgiving, with the facial nerve, the labyrinth, the internal carotid artery, the jugular bulb and the lower cranial nerves lying within millimetres of one another.

These approaches are learned at the dissection table. Each procedure is shown first in an expert demonstration and then practised by every dissector on a fresh frozen cadaver, with faculty at the table throughout. We are grateful to our faculty from around the world, and to every participant for joining us in Seoul.

Course DirectorDepartment of Otorhinolaryngology, Yonsei University College of Medicine

Program

Three days, three regions

Preliminary program. Korea Standard Time.

Day 1 · Wed 12 MayMiddle ear

Eustachian tube & endoscopic ear surgery

Keynotes in the morning, two demonstrations and two dissection sessions in the afternoon.

  • Patulous Eustachian tube
  • Cholesteatoma management
  • Tympanoplasty · Ossiculoplasty
  • Stapes & inner ear surgery
Day 2 · Thu 13 MayFacial nerve

Facial nerve & translabyrinthine approach

Decompression from the geniculate ganglion to the stylomastoid foramen, then the route to the IAC.

  • Facial nerve decompression
  • Translabyrinthine approach to IAC
  • Infratemporal fossa & jugular foramen lectures
  • Course dinner
Day 3 · Fri 14 MaySkull base

Infratemporal fossa approach

A two-hour expert demonstration, then six hours of dissection at every station.

  • Facial nerve rerouting
  • Internal carotid artery control
  • Jugular foramen · Petrous apex · Clivus
  • Certificate ceremony
