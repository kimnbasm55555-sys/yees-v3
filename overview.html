20th YEES & 4th ITFA 2027

Title20th Yonsei Endoscopic Ear Surgery Workshop (YEES)
4th International Infratemporal Fossa Approach Workshop (ITFA)
Date12–14 May 2027 (Wednesday to Friday)
VenueSurgical Anatomy Education Center, Yonsei University College of Medicine, Seoul, Korea
FormatLectures, expert demonstrations and hands-on cadaveric dissection on fresh frozen specimens
Organized byDepartment of Otorhinolaryngology, Yonsei University College of Medicine
Official languageEnglish

Three days, three regions

  • Day 1 — Eustachian tube & endoscopic ear surgery
  • Day 2 — Facial nerve & translabyrinthine approach
  • Day 3 — Infratemporal fossa approach
