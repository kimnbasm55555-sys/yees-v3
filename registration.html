How to Register

How to RegisterTravel GrantsOnline RegistrationRegistration List

On-line registration is highly recommended. If on-line registration is not available, please contact the secretariat at yees@yees.or.kr.

Workshop12–14 May 2027

Registration process

Step 01Register online

Click "Register now" and fill out the form.

Step 02Pay online

Pay the registration fee by credit card or PayPal on the website.

Step 03Check your e-mail

A confirmation with your registration number is sent by e-mail.

  • Your registration is complete after payment.
  • You can register first and pay later by bank transfer.
  • Unpaid registrations are cancelled automatically after the standard registration deadline.
  • If you do not receive the confirmation, please contact the secretariat.

Payment method

Online payment

International credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, JCB, American Express, UnionPay) and PayPal, charged in US dollars. Korean participants may pay by domestic card in Korean won.

Wire transfer

Bank charges must be paid by the registrant. The transfer must be made under the registrant's name. Send a copy of the remittance receipt to the secretariat by e-mail.

등록 절차

Step 01온라인 등록

"등록하기"를 누르고 양식을 작성합니다.

Step 02온라인 결제

홈페이지에서 신용카드 또는 페이팔로 등록비를 결제합니다.

Step 03이메일 확인

등록번호가 담긴 확인 메일이 발송됩니다.

  • 결제가 끝나야 등록이 완료됩니다.
  • 먼저 등록하고 계좌이체로 나중에 납부할 수도 있습니다.
  • 일반등록 마감까지 미납된 등록은 자동 취소됩니다.
  • 확인 메일을 받지 못했다면 사무국으로 연락해 주세요.
