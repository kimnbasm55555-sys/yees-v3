Registration process

1
Register online

Click "Online Registration" and fill out the form.

2
Pay the registration fee

Bank transfer or online payment. Send a copy of the remittance to the secretariat by e-mail.

3
Check your e-mail

Your registration is complete after payment confirmation. Unpaid registrations are cancelled after the deadline.

Cancellation & refund

Any cancellation should be notified in writing to the secretariat. Cancellation before the standard deadline receives a full refund; cancellation after the deadline is non-refundable. Refunds are processed after the workshop.

Invitation letter for visa

Participants who need an invitation letter must complete registration and payment first, then contact the secretariat.
