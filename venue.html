Surgical Anatomy Education Center

Yonsei University College of Medicine
50-1 Yonsei-ro, Seodaemun-gu, Seoul 03722, Korea

The dissection room is beside the College of Medicine building, inside the Severance Hospital campus on Sinchon campus. Lectures are held in the seminar room of the same building.

Campus map — image to be provided by the secretariat

By subway

Line 2 — Sinchon Station, Exit 1, 15-minute walk.
Line 3 — Dongnimmun Station, Exit 4, Maeul (village) bus 8 or 8-1, 5 minutes.

By bus

Bus stop: in front of Severance Hospital.

From Incheon International Airport

About 60 minutes by airport limousine bus (stop: Severance Hospital / Sinchon) or taxi. The Airport Railroad (AREX) to Hongik University Station, then Line 2 to Sinchon, takes about 70 minutes.
